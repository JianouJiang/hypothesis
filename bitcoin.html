This market resolves to Up if the simulated Bitcoin price at the end of the round is greater than or equal to its opening price. Otherwise, it resolves to Down. A tie resolves to Up.

Each winning share pays 1 gold coin. Losing shares pay zero. Payouts are automatic, and unfilled orders expire with reserved gold and shares released at the end of the round.
