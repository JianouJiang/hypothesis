A better test than memorizing a trajectory.

The public games are a practice ground. The seasonal challenge changes the geometry and conditions, so success depends on how well your model generalizes. This is the proposed format; final rules will be published before registration opens.

01 / PREPARE

Build at home.

Use the downloadable geometry and parameters to build your own model. Choose your solver, approximations, and uncertainty estimates.

02 / REVEAL

Meet the new setting.

Everyone receives the new geometry and boundary conditions at the same time. A calibration session lets teams check their setup.

03 / COMPETE

Predict, then trade.

Freeze your model before the scored trials. Forecast on unseen cases and trade during 1–5 minute rounds, with equal gold and compute budgets.

04 / COMPARE

Measure robustness.

Score across several unseen settings. Compare forecasting accuracy and trading results, then discuss where the models succeeded or failed.

Good predictions. Good decisions.

Two proposed leaderboards keep scientific accuracy visible alongside trading performance.

Forecasting leaderboard

Submit probabilities before the observation window. Use a proper probability score, such as the Brier score, averaged over held-out settings. Report uncertainty and performance by case family.

Trading leaderboard

Start each event with equal gold. Rank final settled balances across the same rounds and publish trades with timestamps. Competition balances are separate from everyday practice wallets.

Before you pack your laptop.

What does “offline competition” mean?

An in-person event, with a shared local market running at the venue. Participants bring their own models and trade on the same experiments. Internet and compute rules will be announced before registration.

Will we receive the new geometry in advance?

The practice geometry is public now. The scored challenge uses new geometries and settings released to all teams at the event. Calibration and model-freeze rules will be published in advance; no per-case manual tuning during the held-out trials.

Can I enter without a large CFD workstation?

The proposed format supports reduced-order models and other efficient approaches, alongside full simulations. Equal compute limits and preparation windows will be specified before registration opens.

Is this real-money trading?

No. Predictions use virtual gold. The event’s entry arrangements and any prizes have not been announced.
