The experiment

A steel ball falls through eleven rows of pegs. Trade Yes or No on each landing slot.

How this market resolves

The ball is released once, collides with the pegs, and lands in one of seven slots numbered left to right. A slot resolves Yes when the ball first reaches the floor inside it; all six other slots resolve No. Every winning share pays 1 gold. Trading closes and resting orders expire at that instant. The next ball follows after a short result display. If the ball fails to reach a slot within 12 physical seconds, the round is void: remaining positions receive their cost basis back and unfilled orders are released.

Build your own simulation

Gravity, circular contact geometry, friction and restitution drive the ball; no bin is selected in advance. Small variations in the release position and horizontal velocity create new trajectories each round. The release position varies within ±0.22 m of the centre. This round's x₀ and vₓ₀ stay visible above the board throughout the fall. Download Round data for the full-precision starting state, geometry and release time; y is measured downwards from the board's top, in metres. Motion plays at a slower pace, with each transit lasting at least 75 seconds and full rounds lasting 1–5 minutes so there is time to observe and trade.
