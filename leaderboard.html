    How the leaderboard works

    Profit/Loss ranks gold earned from completed sales and settled predictions, after their cost. Open positions count when you sell or they settle. Your portfolio also includes the current value of open positions, so its profit can differ.

    Volume is the gold value of filled buys and sells. Starting gold and canceled orders are not earnings. Bots are excluded. Resetting your practice wallet resets your rankings.

    Today starts at midnight UTC, Weekly on Monday, and Monthly on the first day of the month. All shows lifetime results since your last reset. Search keeps each trader’s original rank. Biggest wins shows the cost and proceeds of individual profitable sales or settlements.

    In file preview mode, only this browser’s Bitcoin practice results are available. Gold has no cash value.
